Be ready

What to bring to your appointment

  • Social Security cards (or ITIN letters) for everyone on the return
  • Photo ID for you and your spouse
  • Printed copies of your tax forms, such as:
    • All W-2 and 1099 forms
    • Form 1095-A, if you had Marketplace health insurance
    • Form 1098-T, if you are a student
    • Any other tax documents you received
  • Last year’s tax return
  • Your bank’s routing number and your account number, for direct deposit
  • Filing a joint return? Both spouses must be there.

Save time: fill out the intake form first

Filling out the intake form before you come makes your drop-off appointment shorter.

Intake interview form (Form 13614-C)

Filing a nonresident or international student return? Use the nonresident intake form (Form 13614-NR). These returns are prepared only at Heartland Community College.

How it works

Three simple steps

  1. Book a drop-off time

    Choose a site and book online, or call the Normal ARC at 309-888-9099.

  2. Drop off your documents

    Bring everything on the list. While you’re there, you’ll book your pick-up time.

  3. Pick up your finished return

    Our volunteers prepare your federal and Illinois returns and e-file them for you.
