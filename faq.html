Appointments

How do I make an appointment?

Visit our Appointments page to book online at Bloomington Public Library or Heartland Community College. For the Normal Activity & Recreation Center, call 309-888-9099.

Can I just walk in?

No. All sites are by drop-off appointment only. There are no walk-ins and no in-person waiting.

Why can’t I find an open appointment?

Booking opens January 20, 2027, and you can book up to 4 weeks ahead. We open new times all season, so please check back. Virtual appointments can only be booked 24 hours to 7 days ahead.

What happens at a drop-off appointment?

You bring your documents, and a volunteer checks that everything is there. Before you leave, you’ll book a time to come back and pick up your completed return.

What are the hours?

Hours show when each site is open. The last drop-off appointment starts 30 minutes before closing.

What to bring

What do I need to bring?
  • Social Security cards (or ITIN letters) for everyone on the return
  • Photo ID for you and your spouse
  • Printed copies of your tax forms, such as:
    • All W-2 and 1099 forms
    • Form 1095-A, if you had Marketplace health insurance
    • Form 1098-T, if you are a student
    • Any other tax documents you received
  • Last year’s tax return
  • Your bank’s routing number and your account number, for direct deposit
  • Filing a joint return? Both spouses must be there.
Is there a form I should fill out first?

Yes. Please fill out the intake interview form (Form 13614-C) before your appointment. It makes your drop-off shorter.

We’re married and filing jointly. Do we both need to come?

Yes. To file a joint return, both spouses must be present.

Special situations

I’m a nonresident or an international student. Can you help?

Yes, but nonresident and international student returns are prepared only at Heartland Community College. Please fill out the nonresident intake form (Form 13614-NR) before your appointment.

Do you offer services in Spanish?

Limited Spanish-language services are available only at Bloomington Public Library. English-language services are available at all sites. Ver en español.

Can I file without coming to a site?

Yes, with Virtual VITA. You meet a volunteer on Microsoft Teams, upload documents securely and sign online.
