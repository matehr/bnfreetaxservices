Appointments

Book your free appointment

All of our sites work by drop-off appointment only. There are no walk-ins and no waiting in line. You drop off your documents, and we prepare your return.

Booking opens January 20, 2027. You can book up to 4 weeks ahead. New times open up all season, so if you don’t see one that works, please check back.

How it works

  1. Fill out the intake form

    Please complete the intake interview form (Form 13614-C) before your appointment. It makes drop-off quicker.

  2. Book a drop-off time

    Pick a site below. Book online, or call for the Normal ARC.

  3. Drop off your documents

    Bring everything on the what to bring list. At drop-off, you’ll book a time to come back.

  4. Pick up your return

    Come back at your pick-up time to get your completed return.
