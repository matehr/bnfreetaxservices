About us

Free tax help, from your neighbors

The Bloomington Normal VITA Foundation helps families throughout McLean County and beyond prepare and e-file their federal and Illinois tax returns, always for free.

Our work

What we do

We prepare and e-file federal and state income tax returns for families who meet our income guidelines. There is never a charge.

How we keep it free

We’re a 501(c)(3) nonprofit run entirely by volunteers, which keeps costs very low. Donations of money and supplies cover the rest. Clients are never asked to donate.

IRS-approved sites

VITA stands for Volunteer Income Tax Assistance. We work with the IRS and the State of Illinois to run our sites. Returns are prepared for free and without discrimination for anyone who meets the income guidelines.

Our story

Volunteers have been preparing free tax returns in Bloomington-Normal for more than twenty years. In 2011, when our sponsoring organization reduced its scope, longtime volunteer Bill Bartley founded the Bloomington Normal VITA Foundation to keep this service going.
