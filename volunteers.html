Volunteer

Give back with your time and talents

Our volunteers come from all kinds of backgrounds. They share one goal: helping their community. Every tax preparer is certified in tax law by the IRS, and we provide the training for free.

Email us to get started

Ways to help

Greeter & intake

Welcome clients and make sure they have everything they need.

Tax preparer

Prepare returns after completing your IRS certification.

Quality reviewer

Double-check each return so it’s accurate before it’s filed.

Learn more about each role on the IRS volunteer page.
