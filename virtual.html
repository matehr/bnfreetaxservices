Virtual VITA

Free tax help, 100% online

Our trained volunteers still prepare and e-file your return for free. You just never have to visit a site.

Is Virtual VITA right for you? You’ll need to be comfortable:

  • Meeting with us on Microsoft Teams
  • Scanning or taking photos of your documents and uploading them
  • Signing your return electronically. Each person signing needs their own email address.

How it works

  1. Book an intake appointment

    Use our online scheduler. You’ll get a confirmation email with two forms to download and fill out.

  2. Upload your documents

    A second email gives you a personal link to our secure server. Upload your completed forms, a photo of your ID, your Social Security card and all your tax documents.

  3. Meet with a volunteer

    At your appointment time, join the Teams meeting (the link is in your confirmation email). We’ll verify your identity and go over your documents together.

  4. We prepare your return

    A volunteer prepares your return, then contacts you to schedule a second Teams meeting.

  5. Review your return

    In the second meeting, you’ll go over your finished return with a volunteer.

  6. Sign online

    You’ll get a PandaDoc link to sign. For a joint return, both spouses must sign separately, each from their own email address.

  7. Get your copy & we e-file

    Once everyone has signed, you’ll receive your final return in a password-protected, encrypted email, and we e-file it. We can’t e-file until every step is complete.

Why can’t I find an appointment?

Virtual appointments must be booked at least 24 hours ahead, and no more than 7 days ahead. This is different from our in-person sites. Because of this, you can start booking one week before our first day, February 1, 2027.

Virtual VITA doesn’t run on a fixed schedule. Appointments open up as volunteers add their availability. If you don’t see a time that works, please check back. New times are added all season.
